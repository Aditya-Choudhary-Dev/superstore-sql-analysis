# Key Insights — Superstore Sales Analysis

## Sales & Profit by Category
- Technology: highest total sales and profit
- Furniture: high sales but very low profit margin
- Office Supplies: moderate sales, healthy margin but hurt by heavy discounting

## Top Products
- Top 10 products by sales are dominated by Technology (phones, copiers)
- Canon imageCLASS typically ranks #1 by total sales

## Regional Performance
- West region has the highest number of orders and sales percentage
- South region consistently underperforms vs other regions
- Each region's share of total sales visible via window function analysis

## Discounting
- Binders and Machines sub-categories receive highest average discounts
- Heavy discounting is directly correlated with loss-making orders
- Tables sub-category shows net loss — discounting is the primary cause

## Shipping
- Same Day shipping: fastest (0-1 days average delay)
- Standard Class: longest delay (4-5 days average)
- First Class and Second Class fall in between

## Loss-Making Orders
- Multiple orders with 40-80% discounts result in negative profit
- Furniture category has the highest concentration of loss-making orders

## Customers
- Top 5 customers by lifetime sales account for significant revenue concentration
- Customer loyalty and repeat purchasing is visible in lifetime sales ranking

## Time Trends
- Sales grow year-over-year from 2019 to 2022
- Q4 (November-December) consistently shows sales spikes
- Month-over-month analysis reveals seasonal patterns

## Recommendations
1. Review and cap discounting policy — especially for Tables and Binders
2. Investigate Furniture profitability — consider reducing low-margin SKUs
3. Focus retention efforts on top 5 customers
4. Expand Technology product range — highest margin category
5. Review Standard Class shipping SLAs — longest delay impacts customer satisfaction
6. Invest in West region growth — already the strongest performer
